Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

VerifyGlobally (“we”, “our”, or “us”) provides virtual phone numbers for SMS verification. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information we collect

  • Account information such as email address and login provider data
  • Payment-related information processed by our payment providers
  • Usage data such as purchases, selected services, and technical logs
  • Support messages you send to us

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process payments and wallet top-ups
  • To deliver virtual numbers and related services
  • To improve security, prevent fraud, and provide customer support

Payment processing

Payments are processed by third-party providers such as Stripe. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

Third-party services

We use trusted providers for authentication, database hosting, payments, and number provisioning. These providers process data only as needed to deliver the service.

Data retention

We retain account and transaction records as needed to operate the service, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account data by contacting support, subject to legal and operational requirements.
